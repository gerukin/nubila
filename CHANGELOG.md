## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-26

### Added
- First public release preparation: Rust weather TUI and structured CLI.
- Multi-city forecasts, pinned cities, filtering, sorting and responsive tables.
- Hourly/daily detail tables and temperature, feels-like, precipitation, wind,
  cloud and daylight graphs with keyboard and mouse navigation.
- City and period comparisons, historical climate and multi-model projections.
- Configurable cities, local clocks, persistent caches and quota-aware retries.
- Local six-target release packaging, dependency notices and checksum-verifying installer.
